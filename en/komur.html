What does it do?

Kömür is my Python project that turns a black-and-white desktop cat into an assistant. Most of the time it rests in a corner and keeps you company: it watches films with popcorn, dances to music and stays quiet during games and meetings. If you like, it also perches on windows, walks along the taskbar and plays with a box and a laser.

Chat and voice

Local AI through Ollama, with optional OpenAI and Claude. Replies stream word by word. Speech is transcribed on your PC and replies can be read aloud in Turkish. The cat’s sounds are positioned in stereo and it purrs as you pet it. The “Hey Kömür” wake word and the microphone stay off unless you turn them on.

Memory and focus

History, notes and reminders stay on your PC. In Pomodoro mode the cat sleeps while you work and stretches on breaks; a lo-fi focus ambience is optional. It learns your habits and occasionally makes a short suggestion, and reminds you to drink water and take breaks. Your to-dos become fish tasks; friendship points and a level grow over time.

Screen and PC help

Reads the window you choose and helps with it; runs commands such as opening apps, changing volume and taking notes. The right-click quick action panel gives shortcuts to common commands. Actions like closing or deleting ask for confirmation first.

Games and media

Locally recognises video, music, games, coding, reading and meetings and reacts calmly; it stays quiet during games and meetings. Launches Steam and Riot games; plugins can add new commands.

How to use it

  1. Download and run Komur.exe. Close any older Kömür first.
  2. Optionally install the local AI and voice models.
  3. Double-click the cat, type a message and press Enter. Right-click opens the quick action panel.
  4. Try “Not defterini aç”, “5 dakika sonra mola hatırlat”, “pomodoro başlat”, “dans et” or “uyu”.

Local AI works best with 16 GB RAM (about a 4.7 GB model); a light profile of about 2.6 GB suits 8 GB RAM. Speech recognition needs an extra download of about 486 MB.

How it was built

Python and Tkinter for the desktop interface, vector drawing for animation, pygame and Windows components for sound, SQLite for memory. The app is packaged as a single EXE with PyInstaller.

Developed with help from AI tools. The module map and build steps are in the GitHub README.

Security and privacy

Window and media titles are never saved or sent online. The microphone stays off unless you turn it on. Not every feature has been tested end to end on a clean PC.

The EXE is not code-signed, so Windows may show a warning. You can verify the file with its SHA-256 checksum.
